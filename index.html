Survive the night

Clear four waves. Kills earn cash for guns between waves. Climb the watchtower (E) to shoot from above until they tear it down. Streaks earn grenades, a vaporizing blast and a dog.

The four waves

  1. 1
    Walkers
    Slow and clumsy. Shoot an arm and it comes off; take a leg and they crawl.
  2. 2
    Runners join
    Fast but fragile. A headshot from anything heavy takes the head clean off.
  3. 3
    Brutes arrive
    Big and slow, they wreck the tower. Grenades blow them apart.
  4. 4
    Everything, plus The Butcher
    He roars before he charges. When he hits the tower, it shakes.

Kill streaks

  1. 12
    Grenades
    +3 frag grenades. G or right-click; they bounce off cars.
  2. 15
    Vaporize
    Press V: a shockwave turns every zombie near you to ash.
  3. 20
    Dog
    A shepherd joins you, hunts nearby zombies and has its own health.

Your streak survives three hits and breaks on the fourth; the hit count resets each wave. Every 10 kills after 20 adds +2 grenades. Tower damage never counts.

The gun shop

  2. $200

  3. $350
    Assault rifle
    Hard-hitting all-rounder.
  4. $400
    Sniper
    One shot, punches through three zombies.
  5. $550
    Machine gun
    100-round belt. Slows you down.
  6. $250
    Chainsaw
    Hold fire to rev it into the horde. Takes limbs off, never reloads.

The shop opens for 20 seconds after each wave — or walk into the 24/7 Mart by the east fence and press E at the counter any time. It also sells grenades, medkits, body armour, tower repairs and dog treatment. Kills pay $10–40, headshots +$5.

First-person view on / off

First person: click the game to lock the mouse, then move it to look. W/S walk, A/D strafe, ←/→ also turn. Esc frees the mouse and pauses. The radar in the corner shows what's behind you.

Phone or tablet: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires; use the buttons under the screen for the rest.
